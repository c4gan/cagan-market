🛒 **cagan-market | Modern Western Shopping Cart & Store System for RedM** 🛒

Hello everyone! I've officially released **cagan-market**, an open-source, highly optimized RedM store system featuring an authentic 1899 Western dark torn-paper NUI and interactive shopping cart! 🎉

✨ **Key Features:**
📜 **Authentic Western Dark UI**: Beautiful torn-paper grunge aesthetic fitting Red Dead lore
🛒 **Interactive Shopping Cart**: Quantity selectors (`-` / `+`), dynamic subtotal & instant total calculation
🔍 **Instant Live Search & Categories**: Live search bar & category tabs (Food, Medical, Weapons, Ammo...)
🔄 **Universal Multi-Framework**: True auto-detection for **VORP Core** and **RSG-Core** with zero configuration
💰 **Dual Store Modes (Buy & Sell)**: General stores, gunsmiths, butchers, pawn shops, and crop/pelt buyers
📦 **Stock Indicators**: Dynamic or unlimited stock counters
🔒 **Job & Grade Locks**: Restrict catalogs or shops to specific jobs (Police, Doctor, Blacksmith)
📍 **Storekeeper NPCs & Map Blips**: Configurable peds, blips, and smooth [G] hold prompt
⚡ **0.00 ms Resmon**: Ultra lightweight, server-side price & inventory capacity validation
📊 **Discord Webhook Logging**: Full transaction receipts sent to your Discord channel
🌐 **Multi-Language (i18n)**: English, Turkish, German, French

🔗 **GitHub Repository:**
https://github.com/c4gan/cagan-market
